# Ball choices

Paste one block into an image generator. Save the result as the filename under the prompt, into `art/ball-refs/picks/`. Square image. Then say which filenames you want in the game.

Every prompt already includes the shared look: one toy ball, black outline, viewed from slightly above, plain white background, no text.

The dark fold on Paper Core stays out of these prompts.

## What was free to download

These are CC0 (public domain). They are photo spheres, so the grain will shrink away on a hop-sized ball. They are here so you can reject or keep them before any new art is drawn.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `paper/crumpled-white.jpg` | Crumpled white paper. ambientCG Paper 003. |
| `paper/ribbed-cream.jpg` | Fine ribbed cream paper. ambientCG Paper 004. |
| `paper/creased-kraft.jpg` | Creased kraft paper. ambientCG Paper 005. |
| `court/tennis-felt.png` | Fuzzy yellow tennis ball, one pale seam. OpenGameArt, Lucian Pavel. |
| `court/tennis-texture.png` | The wrap for that tennis ball, if you pick the photo. |
| `marble/marble-white.jpg` | White stone with faint grey veins. ambientCG Marble 001. Not teal swirls. |
| `foil/gold-mirror.jpg` | Smooth polished gold. ambientCG Metal 034. Not crumpled foil. |

Nothing free matched Sunband, Confetti, Lantern, Starglass, Prism, or Hopper.
