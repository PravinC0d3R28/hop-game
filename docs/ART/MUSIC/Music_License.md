# HOP music — where the tracks came from

Written 2026-10-08. This note stays in the docs. It is not copied into the game package.

The three loops in `public/audio/` were generated for HOP on this machine with ACE-Step 1.5, model `acestep-v15-turbo`. The language planner was off. Each piece is instrumental, about 75 seconds, and was picked from three takes. Captions and the rejected takes are in `World_Music_Direction.md`.

| World | Take | Shipped file | Generation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sunrise | S3 Crystal glitter, 116 BPM, D major | `sunrise.mp3` | `9e573ad2-66e9-473e-a7cf-461497751ebd` |
| Dusk | D2 Late room, 88 BPM, F major | `dusk.mp3` | `42d24c9a-d27c-4b08-bac8-d858a81f41a5` |
| Deep Void | V1 Open dark, 76 BPM, E minor | `void.mp3` | `a77ff278-9f26-4782-abe0-ef9e0228705d` |

The ACE-Step software is MIT, copyright 2026 ACEStep. The project asks anyone using it to say the music was generated, and to check that a track is not a copy of a protected recording. These three were made from captions written for HOP. They are not covers.

Effects — landings, the coin, buttons, and the rest — are synthesized in the game. They are not audio files.

The software license and the checkpoint's own license are different documents. Before a store or portal submission, read the model card for `acestep-v15-turbo` and keep that page with this note.
