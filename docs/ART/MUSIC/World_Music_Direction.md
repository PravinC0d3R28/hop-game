# HOP world music — direction for review

Draft, 2026-10-08. All three worlds are locked: Sunrise S3, Dusk D2, Deep Void V1.

Stills are the live game, landscape 1280×720, menu hidden, attract camera. Dev save only (`hop_dev_player_data`).

## Already decided

- Three instrumental loops. No fourth menu song, and no singing.
- Each world’s song plays while that world is on screen, including the tap-to-play screen, after the splash is dismissed.
- Play keeps the same song and brings it up to full volume.
- A locked world does not get its song until it can be selected.
- Hopping is not locked to the beat. The pulse can be felt. It must not become a click track.
- About 75 seconds each (inside the 60–90 second range), 4/4, MP3.
- The ending texture should match the opening, with no fade-out, so the file can be trimmed into a loop.
- Each world is generated three times. The three takes are different moods of the same scene. You listen and keep one. The game still ships a single loop per world.

On this PC the language planner stays off, so tempo, key, and length are set by hand. The caption is what steers the sound.

## Sunrise

![Sunrise Peaks, warm tiles over a cloud sea with candy crystals](../../shots/music-brief/sunrise.png)

A pale warm sky, a white cloud sea, and a short run of yellow and orange tiles. Tall candy crystals (coral, gold, cyan, mint) stand off the path. The ball is a small white sphere. The whole frame is bright, soft, and open. The color sits in the crystals and the tiles. The sky and clouds stay quiet.

**Shared mood.** A light morning hop. Bright, airy, low density. The player should feel buoyant, as if the path is floating.

**Leave out of all three.** Vocals, a kick on every beat, a drop, a thick orchestra, a fade-out.

### S1 — Candy bounce

The crystals are the lead. A small bouncing figure on marimba and a music box, a warm electric piano, a thin airy pad, a few soft bells. Toy-like and playful, with space between the notes.

## Dusk

![Dusk District, a purple building canyon toward a peach glow](../../shots/music-brief/dusk.png)

Two rows of purple buildings make a canyon. Warm orange lights sit in the faces. The street ends in a peach glow, and the tiles (cream, with a thin cyan edge) hop straight toward it. Near tiles are clear. The far street softens into that glow. There is no sun disc.

**Shared mood.** An evening walk with a destination. Warmer than Sunrise, with some motion, still cozy. The hop is not a dance game.

**Leave out of all three.** Vocals, a four-on-the-floor kick, a festival drop, horror tension, a fade-out. The cyan tile edge is a gameplay mark, not a reason to make the track icy.

### D1 — Lantern walk

The warm lights are a soft pulse. Electric piano leads, with a muted synth bass, a light shaker, and a few distant warm bells over a smooth pad. Cozy and steady. The shaker is the only pulse.

## Deep Void

![Deep Void, indigo tiles under an aurora, stars, and dark rocks](../../shots/music-brief/void.png)

A deep navy sky, a violet and cyan aurora, and a small crescent. Warm gold stars and thin constellation lines. Dark rocks and tall shafts sit off the path, with a rare cyan edge. The tiles are indigo, each with a pale diamond. The path is a small bright thread in a large dark field. Most of the frame is empty sky.

**Shared mood.** Wide, quiet, and a little tense in the sense of scale, not danger. Wonder first. The aurora is slow color, not an action cue.

**Leave out of all three.** Vocals, choir, horror stingers, drums, a boss-fight rise, a fade-out.

## After you review

Sunrise is S3, crystal glitter. Dusk is D2, late room. Deep Void is V1, open dark. The three files live in `public/audio/` and loop in the game. Slider 100 during a run is 75% of the coin and game-over loudness. The menu is quieter, and game over drops the song to half the run level.
