# Sunrise Peaks — Selected Palette Candidates

These palettes are alternative color treatments for the same locked
Sunrise Peaks art direction.

The following must remain unchanged across palette variants:
- Crystal geometry and faceted construction
- Tile geometry and proportions
- Ball appearance
- Three-quarter elevated gameplay camera
- Cloud sea
- Overall composition and environmental density
- Dark warm outline treatment
- Warm upper-left directional lighting
- Stylized low-poly / illustrated 3D rendering
- Gameplay readability

Only the color system should change.

## Character

Fresh, magical, airy, colorful, playful, and slightly fantasy-like.

## Sky

|---|---|---|
| Sky Top | Pale Lavender | `#DCCFF0` |
| Sky Bottom | Soft Aqua Cream | `#E8F6E7` |

## Crystals

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Coral | Soft Coral | `#F47C82` |
| Peach | Apricot | `#FFB078` |
| Purple | Orchid | `#B58AE8` |
| Yellow | Lemon Gold | `#F8D95A` |

| Cyan | Aqua | `#42CFE0` |

### Crystal usage

- Do not make every crystal rainbow-colored.
- Individual crystals should generally use 2–4 related colors.
- Mix warm coral/peach crystals with cool mint/cyan/purple crystals.
- Use stronger facet-to-facet value differences to emphasize low-poly geometry.
- Large hero crystals should be more saturated than distant crystals.

### Tile rules

- Keep tiles warmer than the surrounding environment.
- Do not use cyan, mint, turquoise, or purple as primary tile colors.
- Tiles should be simpler and less color-varied than crystals.
- Maintain strong top-face / side-face separation.

# Global Palette Rules

## Visual hierarchy

1. **Sky** — softest, lowest saturation
2. **Clouds** — very light and low contrast
3. **Crystals** — highest color variety and saturation
4. **Tiles** — bright, warm, and controlled
5. **Ball** — near-white focal point
6. **Outlines** — consistent dark warm neutral

## Crystal-to-tile relationship

Crystals and tiles should belong to the same visual world, but they
must not become the same visual category.

**Crystals:** colorful, varied, saturated, multi-hued

**Tiles:** ivory, yellow, gold, amber, warm orange

## Saturation

Push the crystals toward a moderately high saturation so they feel
poppy and playful, while keeping the sky and clouds soft.

Avoid making every object equally saturated.

## Facet contrast

Each crystal should have enough value separation between its facets to
remain clearly low-poly and readable.

## Lighting consistency

Both palette variants use warm directional morning light from the
upper-left.

Color palette changes should not alter the lighting direction or
fundamental shading logic.
