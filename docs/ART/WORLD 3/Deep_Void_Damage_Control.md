# Deep Void — damage control

The repair is finished. The pieces below are the world that ships. The camera, the ball, the tile size, the jump, the sway, the unlocks, and the UI stay as they are. Sunrise and Dusk stay as they are. The same seed still rebuilds the same layout. Nothing solid crosses the hop corridor.

## How a piece gets made

1. Build it as one mesh. Displace a shared low-poly hull (an icosahedron, vertices welded, then pushed). Do not stack boxes. Flat color per face, from the stone palette: lighter facets on top, navy on the sides, a few violet faces.
2. Open `void-lab.html?id=...` on the white background. That page shows the game-camera view plus five study views: three-quarter, top, bottom, side, and end. The game view is the angle the play camera uses, from the path, above and behind. Save the sheet as `docs/ART/WORLD 3/lab/<id>-views.png` and the game view alone as `docs/ART/WORLD 3/lab/<id>.png`.
3. Look at every view, not one. A three-quarter spike is not enough. The top must show width, the side must show thickness, the bottom must be closed stone, and the end must not be a paper edge. If any view is a box, a sheet, a hole, or a floating stick, change the mesh and shoot all five again.
4. Stop only when all five views agree with the paintings.

A ring is a thick band with at most three real gaps and a few chips beside those gaps. A gate is that ring, large enough to fly through, facing the camera. A planet is a faceted ball plus that ring, seen at an angle so the ring reads as a ring.

## Where this stands

The four wide rocks, the four jagged rocks, the three seam rocks, and the four crowned rocks are faceted hulls. `crown-planted` is a tall tapered monolith standing in the rock. `crown-gap` is a heavier slanted block with a gap above the rock and a cyan crack on the stone. `crown-twin` is both of those shafts on one rock, the taper taller than the block. `crown-lean` is a heavier monolith tipped over the rock, with a face toward the path and the foot still in the stone. `shaft-tall` is a faceted monolith on its own, tapered, with a cyan edge on the stone. `shaft-slim` is narrower and tapers to a small top, without that edge. `shaft-broad` is shorter and wider, with a cyan edge and almost no taper. `shaft-lean` is a faceted shaft tipped toward the path, with a face still showing. `cluster-three` is three faceted shafts of different heights, the tall one with a cyan edge. `cluster-step` is three shafts in a line, stepping down: the tall one narrows to a small top and leans back, the middle one is a straight column with the cyan edge, and the short one is a wide stub. `cluster-fan` is three shafts opening apart, the near one tipped toward the path with the cyan edge and the far one tipped away. Each top is chipped on the side toward the path, so the rim is broken instead of a flat cut. `marked-hook` is a wide faceted shaft with a warm hook drawn on the face toward the path. `marked-peak` is shorter, tapers more, and carries a cool peak on that face plus a cyan edge. `marked-arc` is a short wide shaft with almost no taper and a cool arc on that face. `gate-break-right` is a thick faceted ring facing the path, with two real gaps and a broken rock beside each. `gate-wide-top` is a larger ring with one wide gap at the top and two broken pieces in that opening. `gate-three` is a ring with three gaps spaced around the circle and one broken piece in each. `gate-heavy` is the thickest ring, with two gaps, one on the left and one on the lower right. The loose pieces taper to a chipped end. Cyan sits on one break of a gate or planet, not on every shard. `planet-twin` is a faceted dark ball with a violet crack, two broken rings, and cyan on only one of those breaks. `planet-wide` is a larger single ring around a ball with a white crack, and cyan on one break. `planet-heavy` is a bigger ball in a thicker ring, with cyan on one break and no crack. `planet-three` is three small balls inside one complete ring, with no break and no crack. `orbit-rock` is a small faceted rock inside a thin broken ring, with cyan on one break and a cyan line on the inner and outer edge of the tube. `orbit-shaft` is a faceted shaft standing through a thin broken ring, with the same cyan edge and cyan on its one break. `orbit-pebble` is a smaller rock in a thinner ring with three gaps, the same cyan edge, and cyan on one break. Each has a picture and a view sheet in `lab/`.
